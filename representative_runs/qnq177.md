However, under this new paradigm, time itself becomes the tension on a cosmic cable. As it pulls, it imparts a specific rotational momentum to these paths. The "straight line" between two stars is no longer static; it is a dynamic spiral driven by the relentless pull of time.

Furthermore, this implies a directional asymmetry to the cosmos rooted in the mechanics of time. We do not just move through space; we ride the currents of a twisting geometry that time actively sculpts.
